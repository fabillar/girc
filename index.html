Who We Are

Together, We Make a Difference

Greater Intracoastal Republican Club (GIRC) exists to empower Republican voices and unite local conservatives around shared values and informed political discourse. We’re proud to serve the Jacksonville Beaches, Queens Harbor, and the greater Intracoastal area.

Members gather for educational forums with expert speakers, campaign events and fundraisers for Republican candidates, and networking opportunities for professionals and community leaders — all in service of one mission: growing the Republican base and turning Duval County red.
